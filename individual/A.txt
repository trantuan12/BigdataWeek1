3. EXIT QUESTION 1 RESPONSE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Question: Why are Kubernetes observer permissions separate from S3 reader permissions,
and why is a namespace operator outside the claimed isolation boundary?
Answer:
Kubernetes RBAC controls management-plane access to the Kubernetes API server (etcd state,
pod inspection, logs). In contrast, S3 reader permissions control data-plane payload traffic
handled directly by the S3 service process (TCP port 8333). Having read access to K8s resources
does not confer S3 bucket permissions. A namespace operator lies outside the isolation boundary
because someone with full namespace-admin privileges can mount Secrets directly, inspect
workload environment variables, or bypass Pod security settings.
