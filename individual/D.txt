3. EXIT QUESTION 2 RESPONSE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Question: Why might concurrency four be slower or have higher latency than concurrency one?
Connect your answer to evidence or a plausible bottleneck.

Answer:
1. CPU Throttling and Contention: In store.yaml, the SeaweedFS deployment has limits:
   cpu: "1" (single CPU core). When 4 concurrent client threads send requests simultaneously,
   they contend for the single core on the single-replica storage backend, inducing heavy
   context switching and request queuing overhead rather than parallel acceleration.
2. Disk I/O Lock Contention: All concurrent 4 MiB object payloads are written to a single
   local-path volume mounted at /data. File system locks and fsync operations serialize disk
   writes, causing queue formation and dramatically increasing tail latency (p95 latency
   jumped from ~110 ms at c=1 to ~686 ms at c=4).
3. Absence of Network RTT Penalty: The client and storage pod execute on the same local cluster
   network where RTT is under 1 ms. Concurrency pipelining benefits WAN workloads by hiding
   network latency, but in a local-node environment with zero latency penalty, concurrency
   merely introduces lock and queuing contention.
