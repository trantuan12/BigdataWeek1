2. EXIT QUESTION 3 RESPONSE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Question: Why does Pod recovery not establish backup, high availability or enforced retention?

Answer:
1. Not High Availability (HA): A single storage replica with Recreate deployment strategy
   experiences an unavoidable period of downtime (10.05s observed) during replacement.
   True HA requires multi-replica active-active or active-passive clustering with automated
   failover and zero data plane interruption.
2. Not Backup / Disaster Recovery: The experiment only proves that data persists across
   transient container lifecycles on the same underlying volume. If the underlying node, disk,
   or filesystem fails, or if an accidental deletion occurs, all data is lost. Backup requires
   independent, off-site, point-in-time snapshotting.
3. Not Enforced Retention: Documenting a retention commitment in governance metadata does not
   prevent an authenticated user (such as the owner) from modifying or deleting objects,
   nor does it enforce WORM (Write Once, Read Many) / object-lock compliance.
